Source: @hijeffdav (Jeff Davenport), "The 60-Second Communication Coach", IG reel DWkbY-YkQ2a, 1080×1920, 24 fps, 65.75 s.
His original edit is a single locked-off talking head with plain white burned-in captions. This version keeps his
performance and audio, removes the burned-in captions, and builds a premium motion-graphics layer on top.

italic (keywords, titles). Glass cards, grain, vignette, gentle grade. Intensity 3 (educational/coach): every
graphic explains the idea; no glitches or flashes.
