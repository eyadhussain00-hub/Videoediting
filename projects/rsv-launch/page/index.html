Routine and rules

Daily, about 15 minutes

  • Reply to every comment and DM within the hour. You sell fast replies, so your own inbox has to show it.
  • Add every inquiry to RSV Studio's own pipeline in the Desk, with "Instagram" or "Facebook" as the source. You'll see which posts bring leads, the same way a client would.
  • On post days, share the post to your story with a link sticker to WhatsApp.
  • On days with no post, put up one story from the list below.

Never say

  • That it picks up WhatsApp or Instagram messages automatically. It doesn't: the team adds each inquiry.
  • Any price that isn't on the Desk. The prices in these images come from offer.ts: Starter EGP 1,500, Growth EGP 2,800, Scale EGP 4,500 a month; websites EGP 4,000 once plus EGP 400 a month.
  • A client name, quote or result that isn't real. Every chat, list and chart in these images carries an "Example" tag. Keep it that way.

Stories for days with no post

Every Sunday: a 10-minute review

For each post that week, look at four numbers in Instagram's insights: reach, saves, shares and profile visits. Then count the WhatsApp chats that came in. Do more of whatever started chats, and change the hook on whatever didn't. One demo call a week from social is a good first target; a booked demo is the number that matters.
